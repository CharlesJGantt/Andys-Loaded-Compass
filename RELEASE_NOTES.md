# Andy's Loaded Compass 0.11.2

A rebuilt location list. Every saved place now tells you where it is, what dimension it is in and what the land looks like when you get there, without hovering over anything.

- The saved-location list is now titled **Bound Locations** and tells you that tapping an entry selects it, so the menu explains itself the first time you open it.
- Each location now takes two lines: its name and biome on the first, its full coordinates and dimension on the second. Nothing is truncated and nothing is hidden until you hover.
- Dimensions are spelled out in bold — Overworld, Nether, End — rather than shown as a single letter, and keep their green, red and purple colors.
- Binding a location now records the biome you bound it in and shows it beside the name, colored to match the terrain: gold for badlands and desert, aqua for snow and ice, dark green for jungle and swamp, blue for oceans and rivers, gray for peaks and windswept hills, dark red for the Nether wastes.
- Coordinates and biome labels were too pale to read against the button and only appeared when an entry was highlighted. Both are darker now.
- The entry the needle is currently following is marked with an arrow.
- Locations saved before this update keep working and keep pointing correctly. They show no biome until you rebind them, which is optional.
