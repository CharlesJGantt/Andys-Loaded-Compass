# Andy's Loaded Compass

## One compass. Every lodestone you care about.

**Version 0.10.3** for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

The vanilla compass can remember one lodestone. The Loaded Compass remembers as many as you bind. Name each place, choose a destination from your personal in-game list, and the needle points there. Your saved locations belong to you rather than the item, so a lost compass is never a lost destination.

It points. It never teleports you.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition **1.26.30 or newer**
- No experimental toggles, cheats, operator permissions, or companion add-ons
- Standard graphics and Vibrant Visuals / PBR supported
- Single-player, multiplayer, Realms, and dedicated servers supported

## What it does

- Bind one Loaded Compass to any number of vanilla lodestones.
- Give every saved location a name, then choose the one the needle follows from an in-game list.
- Keep your locations after dying, losing the compass, or crafting a replacement.
- See each saved location's coordinates and dimension at a glance.
- Remove an unwanted saved entry without breaking its lodestone.
- Have broken lodestones clean themselves out of the list automatically.

## Install

1. Download the `.mcaddon` above.
2. Open it with Minecraft to import both packs.
3. In your world's settings, activate **Andy's Loaded Compass** under Behavior Packs. Its Resource Pack activates with it.
4. Create or join the world and craft a Loaded Compass.

On Android and iOS, open the downloaded `.mcaddon` with Minecraft. For consoles, import it on a phone or PC, then join a Realm with the add-on enabled. Servers and Realms should upload and enable both packs.

## Quick start

1. Pick up an iron ingot once to unlock the recipe.
2. Craft a Loaded Compass with four lodestones, four redstone dust, and one vanilla compass.
3. Right-click a lodestone while holding it, then name the location.
4. Crouch and right-click with the compass to choose a saved destination.

The vanilla compass, lodestone, and their recipes stay unchanged. This add-on adds a separate compass item only.

## Controls and behaviour

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Right-click a lodestone with a Loaded Compass | Bind it and name the location. |
| Crouch + right-click with a Loaded Compass | Open the saved-location list and switch the needle's target. |
| Choose **Delete a location…** | Remove only the saved entry; the lodestone remains in the world. |

Saved locations show their name, coordinates, and dimension: **O** for Overworld, **N** for Nether, and **E** for the End. With no location bound, the needle points to world spawn. It spins when the selected lodestone is in another dimension, matching vanilla compass behaviour.

## Compatibility

Every player has a private saved-location list. The add-on does not teleport players, alter vanilla navigation, or require a companion add-on.

The Resource Pack adjusts the player entity to draw the compass in hand. Another add-on that also changes the player entity can conflict; load order decides which player definition wins.
